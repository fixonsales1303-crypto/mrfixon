Our Mentor & Founder

Mr. Bechar Patel

"Quality is not an act, it's a habit that we've cultivated for over three decades."

With over 30 years of industry experience, Mr. Bechar Patel has guided Mr. Fixon to become a trusted name in display and signage solutions across India. His journey began in 1996 with a small workshop in Mumbai, driven by a vision to provide premium quality products at competitive prices.

Under his leadership, we've consistently delivered products that exceed customer expectations while maintaining the highest standards of manufacturing excellence. His philosophy of continuous innovation and customer-centric approach has been the cornerstone of our success.

Our Journey

From 1996 to Today

1996

The Beginning

Siddhi Sales established in Mumbai, starting with acrylic products and basic signage solutions.

2005

LED Innovation

Expanded into LED displays, introducing outdoor and indoor screens. First major installation for a shopping mall in Mumbai.

2010

Infrastructure Expansion

Moved to a larger 25,000 sq. ft. facility with state-of-the-art manufacturing equipment.

2015

Brand Evolution

Rebranded as "Mr. Fixon" to better serve corporate clients. Launched premium product lines.

2020

Whiteboard Excellence

Launched premium glass whiteboards with Nano Clean™ technology. Received ISO 9001:2015 certification.

2025

Pan-India Presence

Serving 5000+ clients across India with comprehensive display solutions. Team of 50+ dedicated professionals.
