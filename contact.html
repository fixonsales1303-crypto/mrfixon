Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I get a quote?

We typically respond to quote requests within 4-6 business hours. For urgent requirements, please call us directly at +91 83848 58678.

Do you offer samples before bulk orders?

Yes, we provide samples for most products. Charges apply for samples which are refundable on bulk orders. Contact our sales team for details.

What are your payment terms?

We accept 50% advance payment via bank transfer, UPI, or cheque. The remaining 50% is due before dispatch. For corporate clients, special terms may be arranged.

Do you provide installation services?

Yes, we offer professional installation services across India. Our team can handle everything from site survey to final commissioning.

What is your warranty policy?

LED displays come with 1-year comprehensive warranty. Whiteboards have 25-year guarantee on glass. Acrylic products have 6-month warranty against defects.

Can I visit your facility?

Absolutely! We welcome visitors to our Mumbai facility. Please schedule an appointment in advance so we can arrange a proper tour.
